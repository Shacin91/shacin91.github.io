Experience Certificate – Research Assistant

Working for two years as a Research Assistant under the supervision of Sukhen Goswami (Associate Professor, Department of Geology & Mining, University of Barishal), I developed hands-on competencies in coastal hydrogeology and river geomorphology. Through projects on climate impacts on water resources and geomorphic index alteration along the Sugandha River, I learned systematic field data collection, groundwater sampling protocols, and coordinating laboratory tests. I gained practical experience curating and statistically evaluating environmental datasets, interpreting hydrogeochemical trends related to salinity intrusion, and drafting technical research documentation.

Certificate of Research Experience

Working directly under the mentorship of Md. Abdullah Salman (Assistant Professor, Department of Geology & Mining, University of Barishal), I completed a year of intensive academic research. My work focused on collecting, organizing, manipulating, and analyzing geospatial data for two environmental studies: Cyclone Vulnerability Assessment of the Central Coast of Bangladesh and an assessment of drought impacts on agricultural vegetation and production in the Barind Tract using satellite, meteorological, and household observations. Through these projects, I learned to integrate remote sensing, climatic, and survey datasets into spatial models and create publication-ready maps for environmental assessments.
